Correct Answer: Option A — wall

Why?
The word "call" can mean to name or label something. Therefore, the phrase "call a wall" means naming a barrier. The other choices (quail, dull, slate) do not logically pair with "call" in standard English.

Related Knowledge & Tips ▼
  • Synonyms for "call": To name, label, or summon.
    Example: "Call a meeting"
  • Homographs & Homophones: Words spelled the same (homographs) or sound the same (homophones) but have different meanings.

🎯 JAMB Tip

Context Clues: Pay attention to surrounding words; they often indicate which meaning is intended.

Noun Types: Concrete nouns are tangible (wall, slate). Abstract nouns are ideas (courage, freedom). Identify which one the sentence needs.
